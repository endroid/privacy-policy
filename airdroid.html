Airdroid

Privacy policy · effective 6 October 2026
Applies to the Android game nl.endroid.airdroid, provided by Endroid.

The short version

Airdroid does not collect or share personal data. The game works offline and has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDK or app-managed crash reporting. It does not request internet access.

What happens on your device

Airdroid processes your controls, game progress, score and game options locally to run the game. Your three highest completed-run scores and their dates are saved on your device between sessions. This information is not sent to Endroid or to other parties by the app. The game does not provide cloud saves or online accounts.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game sends us no personal data, we hold no game account or gameplay record to delete. You can remove the app and its local data using Android's app settings.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies, for example when you download the game or enable platform diagnostics. This is separate from data collection by Airdroid.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from any player, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play data safety declarations before releasing the affected version.
