The short version

Arte Povera is an Android icon pack provided by Endroid, based in the Netherlands. Endroid is responsible for personal information you send us through support or icon requests. Contact us at info@endroid.nl.

The app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs or user accounts. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions and does not automatically upload information to Endroid. Browsing, searching and favorites work offline.

What stays on your device

Your favorite icons are stored in the app's private storage. Android may keep your search text and scroll position while recreating a screen; this restores your view and is not usage analytics. Android backup is disabled for the app.

The icon request screen lists launchable apps on your device so you can choose which missing icons to ask for. This list is built locally and is not sent anywhere automatically. Arte Povera does not read your contacts, messages, photos or location.

Only when you choose to share

Requesting icons: you choose the apps to include, then choose an email or sharing app and whether to send the request. The ZIP contains only the selected apps' names, launcher components and icon images, plus the verification record described below. Nothing is emailed automatically.

Applying icons: applying the pack or choosing a single icon hands it to your launcher. That launcher keeps and controls its own copy and settings.

Once you send a message or share a file, your chosen app or email provider processes that copy under its own policy. Ordinary email is not guaranteed to be end-to-end encrypted.

Google Play verification

Where configured and available, Arte Povera asks the installed Google Play service for a licence response for this app while preparing a request. Google handles that check under Google's privacy policy.

The ZIP includes Google's signed response when one is available, or an explicit record that verification was unavailable. A signed response can contain the app's package and version, a per-user and per-app identifier, a request number and a timestamp. Endroid receives this record only if you send the ZIP; it never receives your Google password or payment details.

An unavailable or negative response does not stop you from using the app or sending a request.

Support, email & retention

If you contact Endroid, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you send. We use them to respond, handle icon requests and provide related support, never for advertising or profiling. Please do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

Where data-protection law applies, we process correspondence to respond to your request, for our legitimate interest in managing that correspondence, and to meet legal obligations. We keep it only as long as needed for those purposes and reasonable follow-up, or as required by law.

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising or analytics services. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

Your choices & rights

  • Remove favorites inside the app, or clear its storage to reset all local data.
  • Uninstalling removes the app's own data, but not copies already held by a launcher, sharing app or email provider.
  • Contact info@endroid.nl to request access to, correction of or deletion of correspondence held by Endroid. We may ask you to confirm control of the sending address.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and to data portability, and you may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Arte Povera is not directed to children under 13. There is no account to delete.
