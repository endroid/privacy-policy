Arte Povera privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Endroid, Netherlands, provides Arte Povera for Android (nl.endroid.artepovera).

Browsing and favorites

Browsing, search and favorites work on your device. Favorites are stored locally and can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling. Arte Povera has no ads, analytics, accounts or in-app purchases.

Optional icon requests

The request screen lists launchable apps on your device. The request ZIP includes details for only the apps you select, such as names, launcher components and app icon images. Where configured and available, the app asks Google Play for a signed licence verification response for the request. The ZIP can include that response or an explicit unavailable record. The app does not send the ZIP automatically. You choose an email app and whether to send it.

If you email a request or support message, Endroid receives your address and the material you send. Endroid uses it to answer, verify and fulfill your request, and keeps it only as needed for those purposes or legal obligations. Your email provider and Google Play apply their own policies to their services.

Contact and rights

For privacy questions or to exercise applicable rights of access, correction or deletion, contact info@endroid.nl. Arte Povera is not directed to children under 13.
