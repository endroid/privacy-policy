Aura Farmer

Privacy policy · effective 9 October 2026
Applies to the Android game nl.endroid.aurafarmer, provided by Endroid.

The short version

Aura Farmer does not collect or share personal data. The game works offline and has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDK or app-managed crash reporting. It only goes online through Google Play, to buy or restore the full version and to show Google Play's rating sheet.

What happens on your device

Aura Farmer processes your controls, game progress, score and game options locally to run the game. Your highest scores with their dates, your best stage times and ghost replays, your language and vehicle choice, whether you were already asked for a rating, and whether you own the full version are saved on your device between sessions. This information is not sent to Endroid or to other parties by the app. The game does not provide cloud saves, online leaderboards or online accounts.

The full version (in-app purchase)

School Run is free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the other game modes. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. Endroid does not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play on your device whether you own the full version and does not send that information to Endroid or to other parties.

Ratings

After you buy the full version, the game may ask whether you want to rate it. If you choose to, it opens Google Play's in-app review sheet (or the game's Google Play page). Your rating and review go to Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; the game does not see or send them.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game sends us no personal data, we hold no game account or gameplay record to delete. You can remove the game and its local data using Android's app settings.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies, for example when you download the game or enable platform diagnostics. This is separate from data collection by the game.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from any player, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play data safety declarations before releasing the affected version.
