Chromatique
Privacy Policy

Updated 6 November 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.chromatique

A local collection

Chromatique is an offline Android icon pack. It does not automatically transmit information to Endroid. The current app includes no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, separate account, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Optional icon requests are shared only when you choose to send them through your email app; there is no purchase gate.

Information held on your device

Favourite icon identifiers are stored in private app preferences. Android may retain search text, category, favourites-filter and scroll position when the activity is recreated. These help restore your view; they are not used for analytics. Android backup is disabled for this app.

Checks for supported installed launchers happen locally to offer apply actions or instructions. Chromatique does not automatically upload an installed-app inventory or read your contacts, messages, location, photos or advertising identifier.

Selecting artwork for another app

When you explicitly select an icon through another app's icon picker, Chromatique creates a PNG in private cache. It returns the selected icon data to the calling app and gives it a temporary read grant for the PNG. This is not a background upload. That app or launcher controls its own copy and its use under its own settings and privacy policy.

Applying the pack sends its package identifier to a selected compatible launcher, opens the launcher or opens Android home-app settings. These actions do not send your favourites or search history to Endroid.

Removing local data

Remove favourites in Chromatique or clear app storage in Android settings. Clear the app cache to remove temporary exported PNGs. Uninstalling deletes the app's own local data. Manage copies kept by another app or launcher there. Chromatique has no account to delete.

Google Play purchase verification

While your device has validated internet access, Chromatique may ask the installed Google Play service to verify ownership of this app. Android network-state access is used only to avoid checks and reminders when internet access is unavailable. Google Play processes the app identifier, version and a fresh request number with the Google account used by Play under Google's privacy policy. Endroid does not receive your Google account credentials or payment details. Only a fresh, signature-verified non-purchase response may show a gentle, dismissible support reminder. Offline access, errors and inconclusive checks never trigger a reminder. Neither the checks nor the reminder restrict any app feature or icon requests.

Optional icon requests and support

Choose the apps you want to request, then choose whether to send the request ZIP through your email app to info@endroid.nl. It contains only selected launcher components, app names and app icons, plus Google Play verification for this request: a signed answer, including a denial, or an explicit unsigned unavailable record. Signed answers can include a per-user, per-app identifier, package/version, request nonce and timestamp. Google Play processes verification through its service under Google's policies. Requests are never blocked by the result. This pack's actual Play public key is currently missing; unavailable verification does not confirm ownership.

If you send a request or otherwise contact support, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use correspondence to handle icon requests, support and follow-up, not for advertising or profiling. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive material.

Support correspondence is retained while needed for your question, follow-up or applicable legal obligations. Ask for deletion at the same address; we may ask you to confirm control of the sending address. Email providers process messages for delivery and hosting. Ordinary email is not promised to be end-to-end encrypted.

Sharing, audience and policy changes

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising or analytics services. We may disclose support correspondence when required by law. Chromatique is intended for people aged 13 and over, not designed for children. Contact us if a child has sent information you believe should be removed.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. Privacy contact: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
