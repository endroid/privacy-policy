Cinderway

Privacy policy · effective 18 October 2026
Applies to the Android game nl.endroid.cinderway, provided by Endroid.

The short version

Cinderway does not collect or share personal data. The game works offline and has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDK or app-managed crash reporting. It only goes online through Google Play, to buy or restore the full version, and local races connect nearby phones directly.

What happens on your device

Cinderway processes your controls, game progress, score and game options locally to run the game. Your five highest arcade and endless scores with their dates, your best time-trial runs (ghost replays), your medals and destinations, your Daily Run streak and that day's result, the three initials you pick for sharing, your language and vehicle choice, and whether you own the full version are saved on your device between sessions. This information is not sent to Endroid or to other parties by the app. The game does not provide cloud saves, online leaderboards or online accounts.

Sharing a run

When you tap SHARE after a race, the game opens Android's share sheet with a card about that run and a code a friend can paste to race it: your three initials, the score, time, route and medals, the ride and difficulty, the initials and scores of earlier racers of a challenge, and the path your car drove. You choose the app and the people it goes to; the game sends nothing by itself. The game reads the clipboard only when you tap PASTE to load a friend's code, and it accepts a code that you share to it from another app. Codes are read on your device only.

Local races (Nearby)

LOCAL RACE lets phones in the same room race each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, using Google Play services' Nearby Connections; no internet connection or server is used. Only when you host or join a local race does the game ask Android for the "Nearby devices" permission (on Android 11 and older, location access, which Bluetooth discovery requires there; the game does not use your location). During the race the phones exchange your three initials, your ride, your car's position on the road, your checkpoint times and your result, only with the other phones in that race. The connection is encrypted, and nothing of it is sent to Endroid or kept after the race.

The full version (in-app purchase)

Arcade mode is free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the other game modes. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. Endroid does not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play on your device whether you own the full version and does not send that information to Endroid or to other parties.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game sends us no personal data, we hold no game account or gameplay record to delete. You can remove the app and its local data using Android's app settings.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies, for example when you download the game or enable platform diagnostics. This is separate from data collection by Cinderway.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from any player, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play data safety declarations before releasing the affected version.
