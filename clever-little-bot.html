Clever Little Bot privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026. Clever Little Bot is an offline puzzle game provided by Endroid in the Netherlands.

The game does not collect or transmit personal information. It has no account, advertising, analytics, purchases or multiplayer. Your programs, your designed level, completion and preferences stay on your device unless you share them yourself. The app disables Android's automatic app backup.

Sharing is something only you start. When you tap a share button, the game hands a short text to your device's share sheet: a code describing a level you designed or a program you wrote, or a sentence naming the world you reached. You choose the app and the recipient, and that app's own privacy policy applies to the message. The text contains no name, device identifier or other personal information, and the game sends nothing to Endroid or to any server.

The game reads your clipboard only at the moment you tap Paste, to look for a level or program code; other clipboard text is ignored and not stored. It also receives text that you choose to share to it from another app, and treats it the same way.

Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove your programs, your designed level and your progress. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

If you contact us, we use the information you send to respond to your request. Contact: info@endroid.nl.
