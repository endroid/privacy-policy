What the game stores

Clever Little Bot is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect or transmit personal information, and it has no account, advertising, analytics or multiplayer. Your programs, your designed level, completion and preferences stay on your device unless you share them yourself. The app disables Android's automatic app backup.

Sharing levels and programs

Sharing is something only you start. When you tap a share button, the game hands a short text to your device's share sheet: a code describing a level you designed or a program you wrote, or a sentence naming the world you reached. You choose the app and the recipient, and that app's own privacy policy applies to the message. The text contains no name, device identifier or other personal information, and the game sends nothing to Endroid or to any server.

Clipboard and shared text

The game reads your clipboard only at the moment you tap Paste, to look for a level or program code; other clipboard text is ignored and not stored. It also receives text that you choose to share to it from another app, and treats it the same way.

The in-app purchase

Worlds 1 and 2 and the first theme are free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the other worlds and themes. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. Endroid does not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play on your device whether you own the purchase, remembers the answer on your device for offline play, and does not send it to Endroid or to other parties. After a purchase the game may ask you to rate it; only if you tap Rate does it open its Google Play page.

Deleting your data

Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove your programs, your designed level and your progress. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.
