What the game stores

Clever Little Bots is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect or transmit personal information, and it has no account, advertising, analytics, purchases, sharing or multiplayer. Your programs, your designed level, completion and preferences stay on your device. The app has no internet permission and disables Android's automatic app backup.

Children

The game is designed to be suitable for children as well as adults. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either, and it contains no ads, links out of the app or ways to contact other people.

Deleting your data

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes your programs, your designed level and your progress. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, clipboard or location.
