Conveyor Tread Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026. Endroid operates Conveyor Tread (nl.endroid.conveyortread). Contact us at info@endroid.nl.

On your device

You can browse, search and save favourites offline. Favourites are stored in the app's private storage. When you pick an icon, the app can share that image with your launcher through a temporary read grant. The app does not use advertising or analytics and does not require an account.

Optional icon requests

The app reads launchable apps visible to Android so you can choose which ones to request. The ZIP contains names, launcher components and icon images for the apps you select, plus a licence-proof record. If verification is configured, Google Play may be contacted to obtain signed licence proof. The app passes the ZIP to an email client you choose. If you send the email, Endroid receives your email address, the ZIP and any text you add. We use these to verify and handle your request and reply when needed. Your email provider processes messages under its own terms. Endroid keeps request information as needed to handle the request and related support.

You can contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of your request data. The app is intended for ages 13 and up.
