The Family Business

Privacy policy · effective 14 October 2026
Applies to the Android game nl.endroid.familybusiness, provided by Endroid.

The short version

The Family Business does not collect or share personal data. The game works offline and has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDK or app-managed crash reporting. It only goes online through Google Play, to buy or restore in-app purchases and to open the game's Google Play page.

What happens on your device

The Family Business processes your taps, your game progress and your options locally to run the game. Your businesses, money, gold bars, respect, legacy, traditions, recipes, statistics, language and volume settings, and the time the game was last saved (to pay the offline takings) are stored on your device. They are not sent to Endroid or to other parties. The game has no cloud saves, online leaderboards or online accounts.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (gold bars and four one-time perks) are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. Endroid does not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play on your device which products you own and stores that on your device only.

Ratings

The game can open its Google Play page so you can rate it. Your rating and review go to Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; the game does not see or send them.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game sends us no personal data, we hold no game account or gameplay record to delete. You can remove the game and its local data using Android's app settings.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies, for example when you download the game or enable platform diagnostics. This is separate from data collection by the game.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and does not collect personal data from any player, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play data safety declarations before releasing the affected version.
