Glasshouse privacy policy

Glasshouse is an offline puzzle game by Endroid.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, use analytics or advertising, or require an account. Puzzle progress and settings are saved on your device. Android may include that data in your device backup according to your system settings.

Deleting the app's data removes its local progress. The app does not offer purchases or access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Questions: info@endroid.nl.

Effective 24 October 2026.
