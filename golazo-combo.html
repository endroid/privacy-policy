Golazo Combo

Privacy policy · effective 8 October 2026
Applies to the Android game nl.endroid.golazocombo, provided by Endroid.

The short version

Golazo Combo does not collect or share personal data. The game works offline and has no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDK or app-managed crash reporting. It only goes online through Google Play, to buy or restore the full version.

What happens on your device

Golazo Combo processes your moves, match progress, scores and game options locally to run the game. Your three highest match scores with their result and opponent, your win count and your settings (language, difficulty, volume and music) and whether you own the full version are saved on your device between sessions. The game also stores the size of your screen's camera cutout and system bars to keep its controls clear of them. This information is not sent to Endroid or to other parties by the app, and it is excluded from Android backups. The game does not provide cloud saves or online accounts.

The full version (in-app purchase)

The game is free with two clubs. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the other clubs and the hardest level. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. Endroid does not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play on your device whether you own the full version and does not send that information to Endroid or to other parties.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game sends us no personal data, we hold no game account or gameplay record to delete. You can remove the app and its local data using Android's app settings.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies, for example when you download the game or enable platform diagnostics. This is separate from data collection by Golazo Combo.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from any player, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play data safety declarations before releasing the affected version.
