Inkbreak
Privacy Policy

Updated 6 November 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.inkbreak

The essentials

Inkbreak is an offline Android icon pack. It does not automatically send information to Endroid. The current app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, separate account, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Optional icon requests are shared only when you choose to send them through your email app; there is no purchase gate.

Data on your device

Private app preferences keep your favourite icon identifiers. Android may retain search text, category, favourites-filter and scroll position when it recreates the activity. These restore your view, not track usage. Android backup is disabled for Inkbreak.

Inkbreak locally checks whether supported launchers are installed to offer apply actions or instructions. It does not automatically upload an installed-app list or read contacts, messages, location, photos or your advertising identifier.

Icon selection and applying

If you explicitly select an icon through another app's icon picker, Inkbreak writes a PNG to private cache. It returns the selected icon data to the caller with a temporary read grant for that PNG, not a background upload. The receiving app or launcher manages its copy according to its own settings and privacy policy.

Applying the pack sends its package identifier to a selected compatible launcher, opens the launcher or opens Android home-app settings. It does not transmit your favourites or search history to Endroid.

Control and deletion

Remove favourites in Inkbreak or clear app storage in Android settings. Clear its cache to delete temporary exported PNGs. Uninstalling removes the app's own local data. Copies retained by other apps or launchers must be managed there. There is no Inkbreak account to delete.

Google Play purchase verification

When you prepare an icon request, Inkbreak asks the installed Google Play service to verify ownership of this app and adds Google's signed answer to the request ZIP. Google Play processes the app identifier, version and a fresh request number with the Google account used by Play under Google's privacy policy. Endroid does not receive your Google account credentials or payment details. The app shows no purchase reminders, and the answer never restricts any app feature or the sharing of icon requests.

Optional icon requests and support

Choose the apps you want to request, then choose whether to send the request ZIP through your email app to info@endroid.nl. It contains only selected launcher components, app names and app icons, plus Google Play verification for this request: a signed answer, including a denial, or an explicit unsigned unavailable record. Signed answers can include a per-user, per-app identifier, package/version, request nonce and timestamp. Google Play processes verification through its service under Google's policies. Requests are never blocked by the result. This pack's actual Play public key is currently missing; unavailable verification does not confirm ownership.

If you send a request or otherwise contact support, Endroid receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use that correspondence to handle icon requests, answer questions and follow up, not for advertising or profiling. Avoid passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

We retain support correspondence while needed for support, follow-up or applicable legal obligations. Request deletion at the same address; we may ask you to confirm control of the sending address. Email providers process messages for delivery and hosting. Ordinary email is not promised to be end-to-end encrypted.

Sharing, audience and changes

We do not sell personal information or provide it to advertising or analytics services. Support correspondence may be disclosed when required by law. Inkbreak is intended for people aged 13 and over, not designed for children. Contact us if a child has sent information you believe should be deleted.

We will revise this policy if the app's data practices change. Privacy questions: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
