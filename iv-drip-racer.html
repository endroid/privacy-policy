IV Drip Racer

Privacy policy · effective 17 October 2026
Applies to the Android game nl.endroid.ivdripracer, provided by Endroid.

The short version

IV Drip Racer does not send personal data to Endroid. The game works offline and has no accounts of its own, advertisements or app-managed crash reporting. It only goes online through Google Play, to buy or restore the full version. Challenge codes travel only where you share them, and party races connect phones in the same room directly, without a server.

What happens on your device

IV Drip Racer processes your controls, race progress and game options locally to run the game. Your best times and the ghost replays of those runs, the championship tracks you have opened, the tracks you design in the editor, your language and stadium choice, the three initials you sign your runs with, your wins and losses against the initials of friends whose codes you raced, the last challenge code you opened, your daily medals, and whether you own the full version are saved on your device between sessions. This information is not sent to Endroid or to other parties by the app. The game does not provide cloud saves or online accounts.

Exporting and importing tracks

The track editor can export a track to a file and import one. The game only writes to the file and folder you pick in Android's own file picker, and only reads the file you pick there. It does not look at any other files and does not upload anything. What you do with an exported file is up to you.

Challenge codes

A finished run can be shared as a challenge code: a line of text with the track, the ride, your three initials and the buttons you pressed during the run. The game hands it to Android's share sheet, and you choose the app and the person it goes to; the game sends nothing by itself. A code you paste or share to the game is read only when you ask, from the clipboard or from the app you shared it from. Codes contain no name, account, device or location information beyond the initials you choose.

Crash clips

After a race you can share a short clip of its best moment. The game makes it from its own picture of the race, saves it as a GIF in the app's private storage and hands it to Android's share sheet; you choose the app and the person it goes to. The clip shows the race, your three initials and a joke diagnosis, nothing else, and the game sends it nowhere by itself. Each new clip replaces the last one.

Party races on phones nearby (Google Nearby Connections)

Two to four phones in the same room can race each other. They find and connect to each other directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi using Google Nearby Connections, without the internet or a server. Only race data (initials, ride, track, positions during the race and finish times) goes between the phones, and only while the party lasts; nothing is sent to Endroid. To find the other phones, Android asks for the "Nearby devices" permission (on Android 12 and older: location, which Android requires for Bluetooth scanning). The game does not use or store your location.

The full version (in-app purchase)

Challenge mode, racing friends' challenge codes and joining a party are free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks Ghost Race, VS Rivals, hosting a party and the track designer. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing: Google processes the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. Endroid does not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play on your device whether you own the full version and does not send that information to Endroid or to other parties. To do this, the game has Android's in-app billing and network permissions.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game sends us no personal data, we hold no game account or gameplay record to delete. You can remove the app and its local data using Android's app settings. Track files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may process information under their own privacy policies, for example when you download the game or enable platform diagnostics. This is separate from data collection by IV Drip Racer.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this information to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Children

Endroid does not collect personal data from any player, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play data safety declarations before releasing the affected version.
