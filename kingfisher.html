Kingfisher Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026. Endroid operates Kingfisher (nl.endroid.kingfisher). Contact info@endroid.nl.

On your phone

Browsing, searching and saving favorites work offline. Favorites are kept in the app's private storage. Picking an icon can give your launcher temporary access to that image. Kingfisher does not require an account or use advertising or analytics.

Icon requests

Android makes launchable apps visible so you can select which icons to request. The request ZIP contains the names, launcher components and icons of the apps you select, and a licence-proof record. When verification is configured, Google Play may be contacted for signed licence proof. No request is emailed automatically. If you send it with your email app, Endroid receives your email address, the ZIP and any text you add. We use this information to handle requests and related support, and keep it as needed for that purpose. Your email provider's terms also apply.

Contact Endroid to ask about access, correction or deletion of request information. Kingfisher is intended for ages 13 and up.
