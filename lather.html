Lather
Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.lather

The short version

Lather is an offline Android icon pack. The current app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, separate account, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Browsing works offline. Optional icon requests are shared only when you choose to send them through your email app; there is no purchase gate or licence popup. The app does not automatically send information to Endroid.

What stays on your device

Lather stores favourite icon identifiers in private app preferences. Android may retain search text, selected category, favourites-filter and scroll position when recreating the activity. These restore your view, not measure your usage. Android backup is disabled for this app.

Supported-launcher availability is checked locally to offer apply actions or instructions. No installed-app inventory is automatically uploaded. The app does not read contacts, messages, location, photos or your advertising identifier.

Choosing and applying icons

When you explicitly choose an icon through another app's icon picker, Lather creates a PNG in private cache and returns the selected icon data to the calling app, with a temporary read grant for that PNG. This is not a background upload. The receiving launcher or app controls its copy under its own settings and policy.

Applying the pack sends its package identifier to a selected compatible launcher, opens the launcher or opens Android home-app settings. This does not send your favourites or search history to Endroid.

Your controls

Remove favourites in Lather or clear app storage through Android settings. Clear the app cache to remove temporary exported PNGs. Uninstalling removes the app's own local data; manage copies held by other apps in those apps. There is no Lather account to delete.

Optional icon requests and support

Choose the apps you want to request, then choose whether to send the request ZIP through your email app to info@endroid.nl. It contains only selected launcher components, app names and app icons, plus Google Play verification for this request: a signed answer, including a denial, or an explicit unsigned unavailable record. Signed answers can include a per-user, per-app identifier, package/version, request nonce and timestamp. Google Play processes verification through its service under Google's policies. Requests are never blocked by the result. This pack's actual Play public key is currently missing; unavailable verification does not confirm ownership.

If you send a request or otherwise contact support, Endroid receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use correspondence to handle icon requests, support and follow-up, not advertising or profiling. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

We retain support correspondence while needed to handle your question and follow-up or meet applicable legal obligations. Request deletion at the same address; we may ask you to confirm control of the sending address. Email providers process messages for delivery and hosting. Ordinary email is not promised to be end-to-end encrypted.

Sharing, audience and updates

We do not sell personal information or provide it to advertising or analytics services. Support correspondence may be disclosed when required by law. Lather is intended for people aged 13 and over, not designed for children. Contact us if a child has sent information you believe should be deleted.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. Privacy contact: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
