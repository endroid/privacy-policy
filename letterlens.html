Letterlens

Privacy policy · effective 8 November 2026
Provided by Endroid · info@endroid.nl

Summary

Letterlens reads your Wordfeud board on your phone and suggests moves. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. Nothing it reads leaves your device.

Screenshots and the accessibility service

When you turn on the Letterlens accessibility service, it watches which app is in front so it can show its button only while Wordfeud is open. It does not read the content of any app. Only when you tap the Letterlens button does it take one screenshot, which it reads on the device to find the board, the bonus squares and your letters. The screenshot is kept in memory while you look at the suggestions and discarded afterwards. Letterlens never taps, types or plays for you.

If you switch on Keep overlay screenshots for calibration, each screenshot is also saved as an image in the app's own storage on your device, so you can share it yourself to help improve letter recognition. Turning the switch off stops saving; uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the saved images.

Shared screenshots

A screenshot you share with Letterlens or pick in the app is read on the device in the same way and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Word list

The Dutch word list (based on OpenTaal) is part of the app. Letterlens does not look words up online.

Settings

Letterlens stores only its own settings on your device: where you moved its button and whether calibration screenshots are kept. They are not backed up or shared.

Children

Letterlens is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
