Privacy Policy

Little Bot: Bring Everyone Home · Endroid · 21 November 2026

This offline cooperative puzzle game is intended for players aged six and up, including children.

No personal data collection

The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking, purchases, social features or network services. No personal information is requested from children or adults.

Local progress

Campaign progress, selected language and graphics preferences are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Android app backup is disabled. You can delete this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Uninstalling may permanently remove progress.

Third parties

The game uses the Bevy engine and Android GameActivity to run locally. It does not integrate advertising, payment or analytics services. The store through which you obtained the game operates under its own policies.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact info@endroid.nl. If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
