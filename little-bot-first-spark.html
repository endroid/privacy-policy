Little Bot: First Spark — Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026. Developer: Endroid. Contact: info@endroid.nl.

An offline game for ages six and up

Little Bot: First Spark does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. There are no accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking identifiers, online play or social features. The game needs no internet connection and requests no sensitive device permissions.

What stays on your device

The game saves puzzle progress, your current card sequence, interface language and pixel-filter preference in the app's private local storage. This information is not sent to Endroid or to third parties. Android app backup is disabled. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes its saved progress.

Children

The same privacy protections apply to every player, including children. The game has no chat, external links, purchases or data collection. An adult may contact us by email outside the game; information voluntarily included in that email is used only to answer the request.

Platform services

Your app store and operating system operate under their own privacy policies. This game does not integrate advertising, billing, analytics or social SDKs. If the game's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is published.
