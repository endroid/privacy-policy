Little Bot: The Last Seed — Privacy Policy

Developer: Endroid. Updated 21 November 2026.

Little Bot: The Last Seed is an offline puzzle game suitable for players including children aged six and older. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It does not require an account, connect to a server, show advertising, offer purchases or include analytics or social features.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your movement and restoration progress, language and graphics preferences in its private local storage. This information stays on the device and is not sent to Endroid or third parties. You can erase progress using Start over in settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes local progress and preferences. There is no cloud save.

Children and permissions

We do not knowingly collect information from children or any other players. The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access permissions and contains no outbound links.

Platform services and contact

Your operating system or app store may independently process information under its own policies. This policy describes the game itself. For privacy questions, contact info@endroid.nl. If you contact us, we use the information you provide only to respond and resolve your request.

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
