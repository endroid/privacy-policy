Little Bot: Pocket Workshop — Privacy policy

Updated 21 November 2026

Little Bot: Pocket Workshop is an offline puzzle game by Endroid, intended to include children aged 6 and above.

Data stays on your device

The game saves puzzle progress, the current arrangement and language and display preferences locally. It does not collect or send personal information, analytics, crash reports, identifiers or gameplay data to Endroid or any third party.

There are no accounts, advertisements, purchases, social features, sharing features, external links or online services in the game. It does not request sensitive device permissions.

Deleting local progress

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove its local saves. Android app backup is disabled. No server copy is held by Endroid.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@endroid.nl. If you email us, we use the information you provide only to respond to your enquiry.
