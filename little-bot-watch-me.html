Little Bot: Watch Me — Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026. Developer: Endroid. Contact: info@endroid.nl.

Little Bot: Watch Me is an offline puzzle game intended for players aged six and above, including children with suitable adult guidance. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, social features, online services or network permission.

On your device

The app stores puzzle progress, your current demonstration, language and display preference locally so you can continue playing. This information does not leave the app through any app feature. Android cloud backup is disabled. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete local data. There is no developer-held user profile to delete.

Children

The same no-collection design applies to all ages. The app does not request names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or device identifiers. A parent may contact the developer with questions; an email sent voluntarily is handled only to respond to the inquiry and is not a gameplay feature.

Platforms and changes

The operating system and distribution store may process information under their own policies independently of this app. Future changes to app data practices will require an updated policy and appropriate store disclosures before release.
