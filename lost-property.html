Lost Property privacy policy

Lost Property is an offline puzzle game by Endroid.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, use analytics or advertising, or require an account. Puzzle progress and preferences stay on your device. Android may include them in backups according to your device settings.

Deleting app data removes local progress. The app has no purchases and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Questions: info@endroid.nl.

Effective 24 October 2026.
