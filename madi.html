Madí privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Endroid, Netherlands, provides Madí for Android (nl.endroid.madi).

Browsing and favorites

Browsing, search and favorites work on your device. Favorites are stored locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Madí has no ads, analytics, accounts or in-app purchases.

Optional icon requests

The request screen lists launchable apps. The ZIP includes only the app names, launcher components and app images you select. Where configured and available, the app asks Google Play for a signed licence verification response for the request; the ZIP records the response or that it is unavailable. The app does not send the ZIP automatically. You choose an email app and whether to send the message.

If you send a request or support email, Endroid receives your address and the material you send, uses it to handle your request, and keeps it only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Your email provider and Google Play apply their own privacy policies.

Contact and rights

For privacy questions or to exercise applicable access, correction or deletion rights, contact info@endroid.nl. Madí is not directed to children under 13.
