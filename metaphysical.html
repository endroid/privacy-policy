On your device

Endroid, Netherlands, provides Metaphysical. The app has no ads, analytics, user accounts or in-app purchases. Your favorites stay in the app’s private storage. Android backup is disabled for the app. Clear its storage or uninstall it to remove its local data.

The request screen lists launchable apps locally so you can choose which icons to request. Applying the pack or choosing an individual icon hands it to your launcher, which manages its own data and settings.

Optional requests and verification

A request ZIP includes the app names, launcher components and app icon images you select, plus a verification record. Where configured and available, the app asks the installed Google Play service for a signed licence response. The response can include Metaphysical’s package and version, a per-user app identifier, a request number and a timestamp. Otherwise the ZIP records that verification is unavailable. Google handles the check under its privacy policy.

Nothing is sent automatically. You choose an email app and whether to send the ZIP. Your email provider processes that message under its own policy. Clearing Metaphysical’s data does not delete copies already shared with other apps or people.

Support, retention and your rights

If you send us a request or support email, Endroid receives your address, message and any attachments. We use them to handle your request and related follow-up, not for advertising or profiling. We keep them only as long as needed for those purposes or to meet legal obligations. Please don’t send passwords or payment information.

Contact info@endroid.nl to ask about your information or exercise applicable access, correction or deletion rights. You may also contact your local data protection authority. Metaphysical is not directed to children under 13.

This website and policy updates

These pages use no analytics, cookies set by the pages, or third-party fonts. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process standard connection information under the GitHub privacy statement. If the policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
