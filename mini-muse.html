Mini Muse — Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026. Developer: Endroid. Contact: info@endroid.nl.

Mini Muse is an offline dress-up game for players of all ages, including children. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, chat, social features, online services or network permission.

On your device

The app stores your current look, unlocked pieces, hearts, challenge stars and language locally so you can continue playing. This information does not leave the app through any app feature. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to delete it. There is no developer-held user profile to delete.

Children

The same no-collection design applies to all ages. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or device identifiers. Photos in the game are poses on screen only; nothing is saved to or read from the device's camera or gallery. A parent may contact the developer with questions; an email sent voluntarily is used only to reply.

Platforms and changes

The operating system and distribution store may process information under their own policies, independently of this app. A change to the app's data practices will come with an updated policy and store disclosures before release.
