OVERPRINT

Privacy policy · effective 28 September 2026 · applies to the Android app nl.endroid.overprint

The short version

OVERPRINT collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. It also requests no Android permissions, not even internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What the app does on your device

OVERPRINT is an icon pack. Your launcher (Nova, Lawnchair and so on) reads the icon images and the list of apps they belong to from the OVERPRINT package. The OVERPRINT dashboard shows those icons, lets you search them, and remembers your favourite icons in the app's private storage on your device. Favourites never leave the device, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Icon requests

If you tap Request missing, the app reads the list of apps installed on your device that have no OVERPRINT icon yet. It turns that list into a short text of app names and component names, then opens the Android share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app (for example your email app) and send the message yourself. If you do send it to us, we use it only to decide which icons to draw next, and we delete requests once they are processed.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example if a future version adds an online feature), we will update this page and the app's Google Play data-safety section before that version is released.
