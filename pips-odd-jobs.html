Pip's Odd Jobs privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Pip's Odd Jobs is an offline puzzle game provided by Endroid in the Netherlands.

The game does not collect or transmit personal information. It has no account, advertising, analytics, purchases or multiplayer. Your program, completion and preferences stay on your device. The app disables Android's automatic app backup.

Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove your program and progress. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

If you contact us, we use the information you send to respond to your request. Contact: info@endroid.nl.
