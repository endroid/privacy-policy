Pocket Goals privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Developer: Endroid.

Pocket Goals is an offline manual goal tracker. It has no accounts, advertising, purchases, analytics, app-managed crash reporting or internet permission. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information.

Your goals stay on your device

Goals, notes, Pokémon selections, progress, dates and overlay preferences are stored privately on your device. There is no central server, cloud sync or Android backup. Delete a goal in the app, clear its Android app data or uninstall to remove this information. Uninstalling or clearing data cannot be undone.

Floating checklist

Optional permission to display over other apps lets Pocket Goals show a small bubble or list. You start it yourself and can stop it from the overlay or its persistent notification. It does not read, record or capture your screen, detect gameplay, access a game account, track your location or use an accessibility service.

Local reminders

If you set a reminder and allow notifications, Android displays the task and its deadline locally. An expanded notification may show your notes and progress. You control lock-screen notification visibility in Android. Optional exact-alarm access improves timing; reminders can be delayed by device restrictions. After a restart, the app restores local reminders without starting the overlay.

Other apps and support

Pocket Goals does not control the privacy practices of Pokémon GO, Google Play, Android or other apps. If you email support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request and is handled by the relevant email providers.

Contact and changes

Questions: info@endroid.nl. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
