Pocket Goals privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. Developer: Endroid.

Pocket Goals is a manual goal tracker. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or app-managed crash reporting. It does not collect, sell or share personal information, and your goals never leave your device.

Your goals stay on your device

Goals, notes, Pokémon selections, progress, dates, the events you marked as checked, the research tasks you removed and overlay preferences are stored privately on your device. There is no central server, cloud sync or Android backup. Delete a goal in the app, clear its Android app data or uninstall to remove this information. Uninstalling or clearing data cannot be undone.

Events and event research

Only when you open the events or event research list does the app go online. It then downloads public pages and pictures: Leek Duck's field research list (leekduck.com), event banners and reward pictures from Leek Duck's image server (cdn.leekduck.com), and a public event calendar hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Tapping an event opens its Leek Duck page in your browser. These are plain requests for public information; no goals, notes or identifiers are sent. As with any web request, those services receive your IP address and basic device and connection details under their own privacy policies. Endroid does not receive this information.

Purchases

The optional Pocket Goals Plus purchase is handled by Google Play. Endroid receives no payment details from the app.

Floating checklist

Optional permission to display over other apps lets Pocket Goals show a small bubble or list. You start it yourself and can stop it from the overlay or its persistent notification. It does not read, record or capture your screen, detect gameplay, access a game account, track your location or use an accessibility service.

Local reminders

If you set a reminder and allow notifications, Android displays the task and its deadline locally. An expanded notification may show your notes and progress. You control lock-screen notification visibility in Android. Optional exact-alarm access improves timing; reminders can be delayed by device restrictions. After a restart, the app restores local reminders without starting the overlay.

Other apps and support

Pocket Goals does not control the privacy practices of Pokémon GO, Google Play, Android or other apps. If you email support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request and is handled by the relevant email providers.

Contact and changes

Questions: info@endroid.nl. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
