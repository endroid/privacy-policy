Pocket Theatre privacy policy

Pocket Theatre works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or network permission and does not collect or transmit personal information.

Photos and drawings

You may choose an image with Android's document picker or open your installed camera app to take a photo. Selected images are processed on your device into puppets. The camera app has its own permissions and privacy policy.

Microphone

Microphone permission is requested only when you choose to record a voice take. Recording occurs while the app is active and stops when it is backgrounded. The recording stays on your device. You can remove it in Settings or use the app without microphone permission.

Local storage and exported films

Puppet images, poses and an optional voice take are stored in private app storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this project. Exported films are saved to a destination you choose through Android and remain there until you delete them. A destination supplied by a cloud-storage app is governed by that provider's policy. Pocket Theatre itself performs no upload.
