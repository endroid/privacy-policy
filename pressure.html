Pressure
Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.pressure

Offline by design

Pressure is an offline Android icon pack. It does not automatically send information to Endroid. The current app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, separate account, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Optional icon requests are shared only when you choose to send them through your email app; there is no purchase gate or licence popup.

Local information

Your favourite icon identifiers are saved in private app preferences. Search text, category, favourites-filter and scroll position may be retained by Android when it recreates the activity, so your view can be restored. This is not analytics. Android backup is disabled for Pressure.

Pressure checks locally whether supported launchers are installed, to offer apply actions or instructions. It does not automatically upload an installed-app list or read your contacts, messages, location, photos or advertising identifier.

Picker and launcher actions

An explicit icon-picker selection creates a PNG in the app's private cache. Pressure returns the selected icon data and a temporary read grant for that PNG to the calling app. There is no background upload. The receiving launcher or app controls storage and use of its copy under its own settings and policy.

Apply actions send the pack's package identifier to a selected compatible launcher, open the launcher or open Android home-app settings. They do not send your favourites or search history to Endroid.

Control and deletion

Remove favourites in Pressure or clear its app storage in Android settings. Clear its cache to delete temporary exported PNGs. Uninstalling removes the app's own local data. Copies held by a launcher or another app must be managed there. There is no Pressure account to delete.

Optional icon requests and support

Choose the apps you want to request, then choose whether to send the request ZIP through your email app to info@endroid.nl. It contains only selected launcher components, app names and app icons, plus Google Play verification for this request: a signed answer, including a denial, or an explicit unsigned unavailable record. Signed answers can include a per-user, per-app identifier, package/version, request nonce and timestamp. Google Play processes verification through its service under Google's policies. Requests are never blocked by the result. This pack's actual Play public key is currently missing; unavailable verification does not confirm ownership.

If you send a request or otherwise contact support, Endroid receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use this correspondence to handle icon requests, answer questions and follow up, not for advertising or profiling. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Correspondence is retained while needed for support, follow-up or applicable legal obligations. Request deletion at the same address; we may ask you to confirm control of the sending address. Email providers process messages to deliver and host them. Ordinary email is not promised to be end-to-end encrypted.

Sharing, children and changes

We do not sell personal information or supply it to advertising or analytics services. We may disclose support correspondence when legally required. Pressure is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. Contact us if a child has sent information that should be deleted.

We will revise this policy when the app's data practices change. Privacy questions: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
