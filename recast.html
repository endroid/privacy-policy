Recast

Privacy policy · last updated 11 November 2026
Provided by Endroid · info@endroid.nl

Recast prepares videos you choose on your phone and plays them on a Chromecast on your local network. It does not need an account, show ads, or include analytics or tracking SDKs. Recast sends no information about you, your videos or your use of the app to Endroid.

Videos and subtitles you choose

Recast only reads the videos and subtitle files you pick with Android's file picker. It keeps Android's read permission for them, so a recent video or the next episode can be opened again later. When a file is stored in cloud storage, such as Dropbox or Google Drive, that provider's app delivers it to your phone under its own privacy policy. Conversion takes place on your phone; your videos are never uploaded by Recast.

Playing on your Chromecast

While a video is open, Recast runs a small web server on your phone that serves the prepared video and its subtitles to your Chromecast over your local network. Each video gets a new random address, and the server closes when you stop. Like other local casting, this uses unencrypted local network traffic: use Recast on a network you trust.

Finding and controlling your Chromecast uses the Google Cast framework in Google Play services and Google's Default Media Receiver on the Chromecast. Recast gives the receiver the video's local address, its file name as the title, the subtitle tracks and your playback commands. Google processes device, network and session information for Cast under Google's Privacy Policy.

What stays on your phone

To let you pick up where you left off, Recast stores in its private app storage: prepared copies and conversion checkpoints of your last three videos, their names, playback positions, chosen audio and subtitle tracks and cached subtitles; your episode list (file names and their Android file addresses); your subtitle style; and whether the rating request was shown. Prepared copies can take a lot of space. Android backup and device transfer are switched off for this storage. Removing a recent video deletes its copy; clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes everything.

Buying and rating Recast

Recast is bought through Google Play, which handles payment and your Google account under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The app has no in-app purchases and receives no payment information. Once, after a video has played to its end, Recast offers a rating. Only if you tap Rate does Google Play open its review sheet or the store page; Google handles your review, and Recast receives nothing about it.

Permissions

Notifications show that Recast is converting or casting and offer a Stop control. Local network and Wi-Fi access let your phone and Chromecast find and reach each other. Keeping the phone awake lets conversion and casting continue with the screen off. Recast does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Children

Recast is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Contact and changes

Questions about privacy: info@endroid.nl. If Recast's practices change, this policy is updated first, with a new date.
