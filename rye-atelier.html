Rye Atelier
Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.ryeatelier

The short version

Rye Atelier is an offline Android icon pack. The current app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, separate account, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Optional icon requests are shared only when you choose to send them through your email app; there is no purchase gate or licence popup. The app does not automatically send information to Endroid.

Information on your device

The app stores the identifiers of your favourite icons in private app preferences. Search text, category, favourites-filter and scroll position may be retained by Android when the activity is recreated. These are used to restore your view, not for analytics. Android backup is disabled for this app.

The app checks locally whether supported launchers are installed to offer apply actions or instructions. It does not automatically upload an installed-app list or read your contacts, messages, location, photos or advertising identifier.

Choosing and applying an icon

When you select an icon through another app's icon picker, Rye Atelier creates a PNG in its private cache and returns the selected artwork to that calling app. It supplies icon data and a temporary read grant for that PNG. This is an explicit selection, not a background upload. The receiving launcher or app controls how it stores or uses the icon under its own settings and privacy policy.

Applying the pack sends its package identifier to a selected compatible launcher or opens launcher or Android home-app settings. Rye Atelier does not send your favourites or search history to Endroid through this action.

Control and deletion

Remove favourites in the app or clear app storage in Android settings. Clear the app cache to remove temporary exported PNGs. Uninstalling removes the app's own local data. Copies retained by another launcher or app must be managed there. There is no Rye Atelier account to delete.

Optional icon requests and support

Choose the apps you want to request, then choose whether to send the request ZIP through your email app to info@endroid.nl. It contains only selected launcher components, app names and app icons, plus Google Play verification for this request: a signed answer, including a denial, or an explicit unsigned unavailable record. Signed answers can include a per-user, per-app identifier, package/version, request nonce and timestamp. Google Play processes verification through its service under Google's policies. Requests are never blocked by the result. This pack's actual Play public key is currently missing; unavailable verification does not confirm ownership.

If you send a request or otherwise contact support, Endroid receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use that correspondence to handle icon requests and support, not for advertising or profiling. Avoid sending passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

We retain correspondence while needed for support and follow-up or applicable legal obligations. To request deletion, contact the same address; we may ask you to confirm control of the sending address. Email providers process messages to deliver and host them. Ordinary email is not promised to be end-to-end encrypted.

Sharing, audience and changes

We do not sell personal information or supply it to advertising or analytics services. We may disclose support correspondence when required by law. Rye Atelier is intended for people aged 13 and over, not designed for children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent information that should be deleted.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. Privacy questions: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
