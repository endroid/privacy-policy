Sculune
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

Sculune is an offline-capable wallpaper collection by Endroid. It does not require a Sculune account, show ads, or include analytics or tracking SDKs. The app makes no direct internet requests and does not upload information to Endroid. Google Play purchase verification is described below.

Local information

Your saved favourites and a successful purchase-verification flag remain in the app's private storage on your device. Android app backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes that local information.

Google Play purchase verification

Before a purchase has been confirmed on this installation, Sculune checks whether Android reports an internet connection and asks the installed Google Play service to verify the purchase. Google Play handles its own network communication and associated account, application and device information under Google's Privacy Policy. Its signed response is verified locally; Sculune does not retain the response payload or Play user identifier, or send it to Endroid.

Once verified, that installation remembers the confirmation and stops asking Play. Offline use always remains available, including before verification. Connection failures, timeouts and unavailable Play services do not block access. A confirmed online denial can restrict only an installation whose purchase has never been verified; no denial is stored permanently.

Wallpapers and exported images

When you choose to apply artwork, Sculune sends the image to Android's wallpaper service. When you save an image, Android's document picker lets you choose its destination. Your chosen storage provider controls that saved copy; Sculune does not upload it to the developer.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact info@endroid.nl. Any material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy.
