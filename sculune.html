Sculune privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Sculune is an offline wallpaper collection by Endroid. The app does not access the internet, require an account, show ads, or include analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect personal information through the app.

Local information

Your saved favourites remain in the app's private storage on your device. Android app backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes that local library.

Wallpapers and exported images

When you choose to apply artwork, Sculune sends the image to Android's wallpaper service. When you save an image, Android's document picker lets you choose its destination. Your chosen storage provider controls that saved copy; Sculune does not upload it to the developer.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact info@endroid.nl. Any material changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy.
