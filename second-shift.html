Second Shift privacy policy

Second Shift is an offline puzzle game by Endroid.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, use analytics or advertising, or require an account. Recorded action loops, progress and preferences stay on your device. Android may include this data in device backups according to your system settings.

Deleting app data removes the local recordings and progress. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location and has no purchases.

Questions: info@endroid.nl.

Effective 24 October 2026.
