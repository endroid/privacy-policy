Softsignal Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.softsignal

The short version

Softsignal is a paid icon pack without advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, in-app purchases or a separate Softsignal account. Browsing and applying icons work without sending information to Endroid. Google Play verifies your entitlement before enabling icon requests. We receive request or support information only when you choose to send it to us.

Information kept on your device

The app saves favourite icons and request history locally. To offer compatible launchers and find missing icons, it checks the launchable apps visible to it on your device. This can include app names, package/component names and app icons. It does not automatically upload your installed-app list.

Selected request files are stored temporarily in the app's cache. Android app settings let you clear the cache or all app data. Uninstalling removes the app's own local data; copies you sent or saved through another app are separate.

Google Play licence verification

Softsignal asks the Google Play service whether your Google account has an entitlement to this pack. This can require an internet connection through Google Play. The app receives a signed licence response and caches the result for the validity/retry period specified by Google. It uses Android's app-scoped device identifier locally to protect that cache; it does not send this identifier to Endroid.

Google Play processes licensing information under Google's Privacy Policy. Endroid does not receive your Google password, payment details or purchase order ID through this check. The pack does not have direct internet permission, but the separate Google Play service can communicate with Google.

Optional icon requests and support

You choose which missing apps to request. Softsignal prepares a ZIP containing only the selected apps' names, package/component names and icons, together with icon-mapping metadata. It opens the email/sharing app you choose, addressed to info@endroid.nl. You can inspect or cancel the email; nothing is sent automatically.

If you send it, we receive your email address, the request and anything else you include. We use that information to assess icon requests, improve the pack and respond to support questions, not for advertising or profiling. Avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. An icon request does not guarantee an addition or a delivery date.

Your chosen email provider processes the message under its own terms and privacy policy. Transport security depends on that provider; we do not promise end-to-end encryption for ordinary email.

Retention and deletion

We retain request and support correspondence while it is needed to handle the request and follow-up support, or for applicable legal obligations. You may email info@endroid.nl to request deletion of your correspondence. We may ask you to confirm control of the sending email address. There is no separate Softsignal account to delete. Your Google Play purchases and Google account are managed by Google.

Sharing and external services

We do not sell personal information or provide it to advertising or analytics services. Email service providers may process correspondence to deliver and host it for us. We may retain or disclose information where required by law. Links to launcher listings open Google Play or your browser, under those services' own privacy policies.

Audience

Softsignal is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. Please contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information that should be deleted.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and relevant Play Console declarations if the app's data practices change. Questions and privacy requests: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
