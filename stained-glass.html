Stained Glass Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.stainedglass

The short version

Stained Glass is a paid icon pack without advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, in-app purchases or a separate app account. Browsing, searching and applying icons do not send information to Endroid. Google Play checks ownership to enable icon requests. We receive request or support information only when you choose to send it to us.

Information kept on your device

The app saves favourites and request history locally. It checks the launchable apps visible to it to offer compatible launchers and find missing icons. This may include app names, package/component names and app icons. It does not automatically upload your installed-app list.

Selected request ZIPs and picked PNG icons are stored temporarily in the app's cache. Clear local data or cache in Android settings; uninstalling removes the app's own local data. Files saved or sent through another app are separate.

Google Play licence verification

Stained Glass asks the Google Play service whether your Google account has an entitlement to the pack. Google Play may need internet access. The pack receives a signed licence response and caches the verdict for Google's validity/retry allowance. Android's app-scoped device identifier protects the local cache; it is not sent to Endroid.

Google processes licensing under Google's Privacy Policy. The pack has no direct internet permission, but the separate Play service can communicate with Google. Endroid receives no Google password, payment details or purchase order ID through this check.

Optional icon requests and support

You choose the missing apps to request. The pack prepares a ZIP containing the selected apps' names, package/component names, icons and mapping metadata. It also includes Google's signed licence proof: the pack's package and version, licence verdict, timestamp, a request-bound nonce and Google's per-user, per-app licensing identifier. If you send the ZIP, Endroid uses this proof to verify ownership and recognise repeated requests for this pack. It does not contain your Google password or payment details.

The app opens your chosen email/sharing app addressed to info@endroid.nl. You can inspect, change or cancel the message; nothing is sent automatically. If you send it, we receive your email address and the text and attachments you include. We use them to assess requests, improve the pack, verify eligibility and respond to support, not for advertising or profiling. Requests do not guarantee additions or dates. Avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Your email provider processes messages under its own terms and privacy policy. Transport security depends on that provider; ordinary email is not promised to be end-to-end encrypted.

Retention and deletion

We retain request and support correspondence, including any licence proof you send, while needed for requests, follow-up support and eligibility checks, or for applicable legal obligations. To request deletion, email info@endroid.nl. We may ask you to confirm control of the sending address. There is no separate Stained Glass account to delete; Google manages your Google account and Play purchases.

Sharing and external services

We do not sell personal information or supply it to advertising or analytics services. Email providers may process correspondence to deliver and host it. We may retain or disclose information where required by law. Launcher links open Google Play or your browser under those services' own privacy policies.

Audience

Stained Glass is intended for people aged 13 and over, not designed for children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent information that should be deleted.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and relevant Console declarations if data practices change. Privacy questions and requests: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
