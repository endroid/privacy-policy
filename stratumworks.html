Stratumworks
Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026
Endroid · Android app nl.endroid.stratumworks.preview

The short version

Stratumworks is an offline Android icon pack. The current app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, separate account, in-app purchases, subscriptions, icon-request service or Google Play licence checks. It has no Internet permission and does not automatically send information to Endroid.

Information on your device

The app stores the identifiers of your favourite icons in private app preferences. Search text, category, favourites-filter and scroll position may be retained by Android when the activity is recreated. These are used to restore your view, not for analytics. Android backup is disabled for this app.

The app checks locally whether supported launchers are installed to offer apply actions or instructions. It does not upload an installed-app list or read your contacts, messages, location, photos or advertising identifier.

Choosing and applying an icon

When you select an icon through another app's icon picker, Stratumworks creates a PNG in its private cache and returns the selected artwork to that calling app. It supplies icon data and a temporary read grant for that PNG. This is an explicit selection, not a background upload. The receiving launcher or app controls how it stores or uses the icon under its own settings and privacy policy.

Applying the pack sends its package identifier to a selected compatible launcher or opens launcher or Android home-app settings. Stratumworks does not send your favourites or search history to Endroid through this action.

Control and deletion

Remove favourites in the app or clear app storage in Android settings. Clear the app cache to remove temporary exported PNGs. Uninstalling removes the app's own local data. Copies retained by another launcher or app must be managed there. There is no Stratumworks account to delete.

Optional support correspondence

The app does not generate or automatically send support emails or request ZIPs. If you independently email info@endroid.nl, Endroid receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use that correspondence to answer questions, not for advertising or profiling. Avoid sending passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

We retain correspondence while needed for support and follow-up or applicable legal obligations. To request deletion, contact the same address; we may ask you to confirm control of the sending address. Email providers process messages to deliver and host them. Ordinary email is not promised to be end-to-end encrypted.

Sharing, audience and changes

We do not sell personal information or supply it to advertising or analytics services. We may disclose support correspondence when required by law. Stratumworks is intended for people aged 13 and over, not designed for children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent information that should be deleted.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. Privacy questions: Endroid, info@endroid.nl.
