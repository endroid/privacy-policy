The short version

Thumbprint is an Android icon pack provided by Endroid, based in the Netherlands. Endroid is responsible for personal information you send us through support or icon requests. Contact us at info@endroid.nl.

The app has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs or user accounts. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and does not automatically upload information to Endroid. Browsing, searching and favourites work offline.

What stays on your device

Favourite icon identifiers are stored in the app's private preferences. Android may retain your search text, selected category, favourites filter, scroll position and request selection when recreating a screen. These settings restore your view; they are not usage analytics. Android backup is disabled for the app.

The app checks installed launchers and launchable apps locally to offer compatible apply actions and let you select missing icons to request. No installed-app inventory is automatically sent to Endroid. Thumbprint does not read your contacts, messages, photos or location.

Only when you choose to share

Choosing an icon: selecting an icon through another app's picker creates a PNG in Thumbprint's private cache and returns the selected icon to the calling app with temporary read access. Applying the pack opens the selected launcher or Android home-app settings. The receiving app controls its own copy and settings.

Requesting icons: you choose the apps to include, then choose a sharing or email app and whether to send the request. The ZIP contains those selected app names, launcher components and icon images, plus the verification record described below. Nothing is emailed automatically, and there is no purchase gate for preparing or sharing a request.

Once you send a message or share a file, your chosen app or email provider processes that copy under its own policy. Ordinary email is not guaranteed to be end-to-end encrypted.

Google Play verification

When preparing a request, Thumbprint asks the installed Google Play service for a licence response for this app. Google handles that check through its service under Google's privacy policy.

The ZIP retains Google's signed response when available, including a denial, or an explicit unsigned record that verification was unavailable. A signed response can contain the app's package and version, a per-user/per-app identifier, a request number and a timestamp. The request number is derived from the selected-app mapping. Endroid receives this record only if you choose to send the ZIP; it does not receive your Google password or payment details.

An unavailable or negative response does not prevent you from using the app or sharing an icon request. Unavailable verification is not proof of a purchase or of piracy.

Support, email & retention

If you contact Endroid, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, handle icon requests and provide related support, not for advertising or profiling. Please do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

Where data-protection law applies, we process correspondence as needed to respond to your request or provide support, for our legitimate interest in managing that correspondence, and to meet legal obligations where applicable. We keep it only as long as needed for those purposes and reasonable follow-up, or as required by law.

Email providers process messages for delivery and hosting. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising or analytics services. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

Your choices & rights

  • Remove favourites inside the app. Clear its cache to remove temporary exports and request ZIPs, or clear app storage to reset local data.
  • Uninstalling removes the app's own local data, but cannot remove copies already held by a launcher, sharing app or email provider.
  • Contact info@endroid.nl to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence held by Endroid. We may ask you to confirm control of the sending address.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and to data portability. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. There is no Thumbprint account to delete. If a child has sent information that you believe should be removed, please contact us.

This website & policy updates

These legal pages load no third-party fonts, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process standard connection information, such as your IP address, under the GitHub privacy statement. Website hosting is separate from the Android app's local processing.

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its date, and provide any notice required by law.
