Thumbprint Privacy Policy

Updated 12 November 2026 · Endroid · nl.endroid.thumbprint

Thumbprint is an offline Android icon pack. It has no advertisements, analytics, tracking SDKs, accounts, in-app purchases or subscriptions. It does not automatically send information to Endroid.

On your device

Favourite icon identifiers stay in private app preferences. Android may restore search text, categories and scroll position when recreating the activity. Backup is disabled. Compatible launchers and installed apps are queried locally for applying icons and selecting optional requests. Thumbprint does not read your contacts, messages, photos or location.

Choosing icons

Using another app's icon picker returns your selected icon as a cached PNG with a temporary read grant. Applying the pack opens a compatible launcher or Android home settings. The receiving app controls its copy and settings.

Optional requests

You choose which installed apps to request and whether to share the resulting ZIP through your email app. The ZIP contains selected app names, launcher components and icon images, plus Google's signed Play verification response when available, including a denial, or an explicit unavailable record. There is no purchase gate. Nothing is emailed automatically. Your email provider processes messages under its own policy. If you send a request to Endroid, its contents and your email are used to answer and fulfil it; ask for deletion by contacting us.

Your choices

Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local favourites and cached data. Copies shared with another app or by email are governed by that service. Contact info@endroid.nl about privacy, access or deletion. Where applicable, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.
