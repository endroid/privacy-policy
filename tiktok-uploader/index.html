Endroid Studio Uploader

updated 25 November 2026 · Endroid Studio Uploader, provided by Endroid

Endroid Studio Uploader is a small desktop tool that Endroid uses to send its own game and icon-pack trailers to its own TikTok account as drafts. Each draft arrives in the TikTok inbox, where it is reviewed, captioned and posted by hand in the TikTok app. The tool never posts anything by itself.
