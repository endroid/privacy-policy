Endroid Studio Uploader

Privacy policy · updated 25 November 2026
Applies to the desktop tool Endroid Studio Uploader, provided by Endroid.

The short version

The tool is used only by Endroid, on Endroid's own computer, to upload Endroid's own promotional videos to Endroid's own TikTok account. It has no other users and collects no personal data from anyone.

What the tool uses

When the TikTok account owner signs in with TikTok, the tool asks for the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. TikTok returns an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID. These are stored only in a private file on Endroid's computer and are used only to check which account is connected and to send video drafts to that account's TikTok inbox.

The only content sent to TikTok is the video file that Endroid chose to upload. The tool does not read followers, comments, messages, viewing data or any other TikTok data.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Videos are processed by TikTok under TikTok's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

The tokens are kept until the account owner disconnects the tool, which deletes them. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
