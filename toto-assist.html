Toto Assist

Privacy policy · effective 9 November 2026
Provided by Endroid · info@endroid.nl

Summary

Toto Assist lists Unibet's upcoming football odds, remembers the bets you tick and can notify you about new high odds. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your bets and settings stay on your phone.

Odds requests

To load the odds, the app downloads Unibet's public list of upcoming football matches from Kambi's servers (eu-offering-api.kambicdn.com), without any account, identifier or information about your bets. Like any website, that server receives your IP address and the app's name and version with the request. Toto Assist makes one such request when its saved copy is older than your check interval, when you tap refresh or Check now, and during background checks while alerts are on.

Stored on your device

The app saves the latest odds list, the outcomes you tick (match, pick, odds and kick-off time) and your alert settings in its own storage on the phone. Bets disappear two weeks after kick-off, or earlier when you remove them. Toto Assist does not fetch or store scores or results. None of this is backed up or shared; uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Notifications

Notifications are created on the phone itself: new high odds from the downloaded odds, and kick-off reminders from the bets you ticked. No push service or server of Endroid is involved.

Children

Toto Assist is about sports betting odds and is meant for adults. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
