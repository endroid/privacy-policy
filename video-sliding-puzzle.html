Video Sliding Puzzle

Privacy policy · effective 20 October 2026
Android game nl.endroid.videoslidingpuzzle, provided by Endroid.

The short version

Video Sliding Puzzle is a free sliding puzzle with no advertisements, purchases, accounts, analytics, advertising identifier or app-managed crash reporting. It has no internet permission. Endroid does not receive your photos, videos, camera frames or scores.

Your photos and videos

You choose a photo or video using Android's document picker, or take a photo with your camera app. Video Sliding Puzzle opens only the item you select; it does not scan your library or request library-wide storage access. Images are processed on your device and original files are not modified. Working photos and shared-result images use the app's private cache. Android can clear this cache, and you can clear it or uninstall the app in Android settings. Video frames and puzzle progress are held in memory; there is no cloud save.

Camera permission

Android asks for camera permission when you choose a camera mode. You can refuse or revoke it in Android settings and still open your own media. Live camera frames are processed on your device, not recorded or uploaded. Camera access and video playback stop while the app is in the background.

Sharing a result

Only when you press Share after solving a puzzle, Video Sliding Puzzle prepares a still image of the completed screen, including its time and move count, and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is automatically sent. The app you choose receives the image and score if you complete that sharing action, under that app's privacy policy. Check that your picture contains nothing private before sharing it.

Children and families

The same local-only processing applies to players of every age. Video Sliding Puzzle does not request a name, date of birth, email address, contacts or location and does not provide chat, public profiles, an online gallery or third-party advertising. Parents can manage camera permissions and installed sharing apps using Android's parental controls. Avoid including private information in pictures you share.

Android and Google Play

Android, Google Play, your camera app, document providers and apps you share with may process information under their own policies, separately from Video Sliding Puzzle.

Contact and changes

Contact Endroid at info@endroid.nl. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you send and use it to answer you. Do not include sensitive pictures or children's personal information. Ask us to delete your correspondence at any time. We update the effective date when this policy changes.
